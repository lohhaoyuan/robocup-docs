A curated collection of valuable resources to help you learn more about RoboCup, robotics, and autonomous systems.

## YouTube Channels

- **RoboCup Official** - Official RoboCup competitions and announcements
- **FIRST Robotics** - High school robotics competition content
- **Robotics Back-End** - Advanced robotics tutorials and explanations
- **Sentdex** - Machine learning and robotics for beginners

## Reading Materials

### Books
- *Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots* - Roland Siegwart & Illah R. Nourbakhsh
- *Probabilistic Robotics* - Thrun, Burgard & Fox
- *Planning Algorithms* - Steven M. LaValle

### Papers & Articles
- IEEE Xplore Digital Library
- arXiv Computer Science papers
- RoboCup League-specific technical papers

### Websites
- [RoboCup Official Website](https://www.robocup.org/)
- [ROS (Robot Operating System)](https://www.ros.org/)
- [OpenCV Documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/)
