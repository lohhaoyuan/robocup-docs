## Questionable Authorship

Parts of this document were drafted with ordinary human care: a bit of planning, a bit of guessing, and the occasional confident statement that probably deserved more checking. At least, that’s how it appears on the surface.

Some paragraphs were revised thoughtfully, others were left in their first form, and a few lines seem almost too polished, as if they were written by someone trying very hard to sound consistently sensible. Readers are encouraged to take each sentence as it comes; precision may vary.

A useful rule of thumb: passages that flow smoothly and stay oddly on-track may not be the result of human hands. Humans tend to drift, hesitate, contradict themselves slightly. Their paragraphs curve in unexpected directions. Mine—well, the pieces that weren’t touched—have a certain structure to them. Balanced. Predictable. Calm. Almost like they were generated.
